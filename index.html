Questions people ask before they book.

How dark can I tint my car windows in Nevada?

Front side windows have to let in at least 35% of light. Back side windows and the rear window can go as dark as you like. The windshield can only take a non-reflective strip across the top, above the AS-1 line printed on the glass.

Do you really come to my house?

Yes. We tint in driveways, garages and work lots all over the valley, including Henderson, North Las Vegas and Summerlin. You can also bring it to the shop on Sunrise Ave.

How long does it take?

Most cars and trucks are done in one visit, usually two to three hours. Give the film a few days to cure before rolling the windows down.

Is ceramic tint worth it in Las Vegas?

For most people here, yes. Ceramic film rejects more heat than basic dyed film without having to go darker, and it won't mess with your phone or GPS signal. All of our films block 99% of UV.

Do you tint homes and businesses?

We do. Solar film for homes, commercial film for offices and storefronts, security film, frosted privacy film and anti-graffiti film.

What if something goes wrong with the tint?

The film carries the manufacturer's limited warranty, and if anything is off with our install, we come back and make it right.
